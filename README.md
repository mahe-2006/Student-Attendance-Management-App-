The Student Attendance Management App is a user-friendly digital system developed to manage and monitor student attendance efficiently. It helps teachers record daily attendance quickly and maintain accurate attendance records without using traditional paper registers. The application allows teachers to mark students as present, absent, or late and view attendance history whenever required. It can also calculate individual attendance percentages and generate monthly or semester reports. Administrators can manage student details, classes, teachers, and attendance records from a centralized dashboard. Students can view their attendance status and track their attendance percentage. The system reduces paperwork, saves time, minimizes errors, and makes attendance management more organized and transparent. Overall, the app provides a simple, secure, and efficient solution for educational institutions to manage student attendance.
